**Status:** Draft for product and legal review; not approved for public launch

**Important:** This is a product-planning draft, not legal advice. Do not publish it as final until qualified counsel has reviewed it for the jurisdictions in which Smart BethG will operate.

## 1. Service description
Smart BethG is an AI-assisted software service that may provide conversational assistance, research, memory, file processing, project/workflow management, content drafting, and user-authorized tools. Features may be introduced, changed, limited, or discontinued as the service evolves.

## 2. Accounts and security
Users must provide accurate account information, protect their credentials, and notify the operator of suspected unauthorized access. Users must not access another user's account, workspace, files, memories, provider credentials, or generated artifacts without authorization.

## 3. AI output and user review
AI-generated responses, summaries, code, research, and content can be incomplete or incorrect. Users must review outputs before relying on them, publishing them, executing generated code, or using them for consequential decisions. The service does not guarantee that outputs are accurate, original, safe, or suitable for a particular purpose.

## 4. Tools and external actions
Some features may interact with files, repositories, terminals, networks, external providers, or other services. The service should explain material actions and request approval where required. Users must not authorize actions they do not understand or have permission to perform. Approval is limited to the specific action presented and must not be treated as blanket authorization.

## 5. Acceptable use
Users must not use Smart BethG to violate law, infringe rights, distribute malware, bypass access controls, abuse external services, harass others, or attempt to access other users' data. This section requires legal review and more specific abuse-reporting and enforcement procedures before publication.

## 6. User content and ownership
Users retain rights they hold in content they submit, subject to the permissions needed to operate the service. The final terms must specify the narrow service-processing license, retention rules, treatment of uploaded files and generated artifacts, third-party provider handling, and the effect of account deletion.

## 7. Third-party providers
Smart BethG may connect to external AI providers, search providers, storage providers, or deployment services. Their own terms and privacy policies may apply. The final terms must list relevant providers and explain that availability, model behavior, pricing, and data handling can differ by provider.

## 8. Availability, changes, and termination
The service may experience outages, maintenance, limits, or changes. The final terms must specify account suspension, termination, appeals, data export, deletion, and any service-level commitments; no service-level commitment is made by this draft.

This section requires jurisdiction-specific legal drafting. Do not rely on this placeholder as an enforceable disclaimer or limitation of liability.
