# Mentor - "Learn as you build" (agents: read before changing AI replies, Settings or onboarding)

## What it is
Mentor is the product's core promise: people learn while they build. It is ON by default for everyone. Settings > Learning > Mentor has an on/off switch. Never remove the switch. Never turn it off for a user. Never block building behind a lesson.

## Where the slogan shows
"Learn as you build" appears under the app name on splash, onboarding slide 1, sign-up, and the Home top bar.

## What Mentor adds to a build (collapsed by default, one tap to open)
1. What I built, in plain words.
2. Why it is built this way.
3. Quality check: Works / Readable / Can grow, with one short reason and a "Show me" cleaner version. These are suggestions, not rules. The AI can be wrong, so link to official docs when explaining a fact.
4. Quick check: one optional question about the code just written. Always skippable.
5. Try it yourself: a small task with Hint, Bigger hint, Show solution.
6. "Hide for this reply" on every card.

## Rules
- Cards are short (a few lines). Mentor must never make a reply feel slow or preachy for experts.
- Mentor appears in chat, the Lab IDE (Explain action, lightbulb in the gutter) and Preview. It also covers hardware: why a resistor value, what a pin map means.
- Progress lives in Settings > What you've learned (concepts, level, recent lessons). Do not use streaks, guilt, or pressure.
- Mentor costs extra tokens. Keep cards short and count them in credits.
- Do not claim Mentor makes anyone an expert. Measure whether it helps before promising results.
- Later: link lessons to practice challenges in the arena app.
