# Website Rules (agents: read before editing the marketing site)

Reference: smartbethg-website.html.

## Purpose
One scrolling page that explains the app, then gets people to sign up. Registration is always open. No invite codes.

## Navigation
- Sticky top bar: logo + "Learn as you build", a Sign up button, and a three-line menu button.
- Menu groups collapse: Product (Overview, Mentor, Lab, Connectors and more), Documentation (Getting started, Mentor guide, Lab guide, Connectors guide, Examples, FAQ), Pricing and credits, Dashboard, then Sign in and Sign up free.
- Dashboard: if the visitor is not signed up or signed in, show the "Open your dashboard" sign-up prompt. Signed-in users go straight to the app.
- On desktop the menu opens as a side panel; on phones it fills the screen.

## Page order
Hero ("Learn as you build", Sign up free, See how it works, app pictures) > How it works (3 steps) > Everything in one place (6 cards) > Mentor spotlight > Lab spotlight > Open to everyone band > FAQ > Footer.

## Content rules
- Pictures are real app screens. Never show features that do not exist.
- Be honest: say simulation is a check, not a guarantee. Do not invent prices, numbers or user counts.
- Mention that Mentor is on by default and can be switched off.
- Keep the slogan "Learn as you build" in the top bar and footer.
- Documentation pages must exist before they are linked; until then keep links marked as coming soon.
