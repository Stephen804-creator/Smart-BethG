Data & privacy

Your account

Smart BethG's initial account policy is 18+. Account eligibility is based on the current Terms and your confirmation during registration or policy review.

Saved chats

Regular conversations are stored in your Smart BethG history and appear in Recents. Temporary chats are not saved to Smart BethG conversation history or product exports. The transcript exists only in browser memory and is cleared on refresh or when you leave the page. Messages still go to the selected AI provider for a response, and that provider may process them under its own terms.

Uploaded files

Files uploaded through the composer are saved in your file library. They are included as source context only when attached to a chat; uploading does not automatically add them to persistent knowledge.
