Privacy Policy

Effective date: This is a product-facing draft for implementation review and must be validated by qualified counsel before publication.

Smart BethG collects the information needed to create and operate your account, authenticate you, maintain security, and provide the AI service. This may include your username, password hash, session state, request logs, contact details if provided, prompts and AI outputs, uploaded files, and usage metadata.

We use this information to provide the service, prevent abuse, improve reliability, protect security, and enforce account and usage policies. Some requests may be routed to third-party AI providers for generation or related processing as part of the service. We keep those provider relationships limited to the minimum required for operation and we do not intentionally share passwords or secret keys with them.

We retain account, security, and usage records as needed to operate the service, enforce policies, and comply with legal obligations. Some data may be kept in backups or logs for operational reasons. We do not sell personal data.

You may request to access, correct, or delete your account and associated personal data where applicable, subject to legal, security, and operational exceptions. Deletion can be limited by legal requirements, fraud prevention, evidence retention, or the operational need to keep records required to protect the service.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no service can guarantee absolute security. Please do not upload or transmit sensitive personal information that you would not be comfortable exposing to an AI service or third-party infrastructure provider.

We may update this Privacy Policy when features or data practices change. If we materially change how we use personal data or third-party providers, we will provide notice and request renewed consent where required.

Users must be at least 15 years old to create an account and use the service.
